Privacy Policy — OBD Scanner

Last updated: March 3, 2026

1. Introduction

OBD Scanner is an Android application that helps you read vehicle diagnostic data via OBD-II adapters. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Contact: alexei.pritchin@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

We collect only anonymous, aggregated data to improve the app and understand usage patterns:

  • VIN anonymization: First 3 characters (WMI) and year code character — never the full VIN. Used for analytics (e.g., popular makes/years).
  • Scan analytics: Aggregated metrics such as number of error codes, protocol used, scan status, timing — no individual DTC codes.
  • Firebase Analytics: Events like obd_vin_read, obd_dtc_scan, obd_static_error — anonymized usage events.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reports and stack traces for debugging — no personal data included.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Full VIN
  • Location or GPS data
  • Personal data (name, email, phone number)
  • Persistent identifiers that could identify you

4. Purpose

We use the collected data to:

  • Improve app reliability and performance
  • Identify and fix crashes
  • Understand usage patterns (e.g., popular protocols, features)
  • Prioritize development based on real-world usage

5. Storage and Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on Google Firebase servers. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to know what data is collected. The app does not collect personal information. If you have questions or concerns, contact us at alexei.pritchin@gmail.com.

7. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest changes.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions: alexei.pritchin@gmail.com
